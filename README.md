## 📌 Overview

**CBT Mastery Hub** bridges the gap between passive reading and high-pressure exam execution. Built for competitive exams requiring strict time discipline, it combines **24 structured subject modules** with a zero-friction **Computer-Based Testing (CBT) engine** modeled after official testing interfaces.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📖 Notes-Based Learning
* **24 Exhaustive Chapters** spanning General Awareness, General Science, English Language, and Mathematics.
* High-yield key takeaways, highlighted formula blocks, and concept maps for rapid revision.

### ⭐ Chapter Mastery Quizzes
* Targeted 10-question evaluation at the end of every chapter.
* Instant 3-tier mastery scoring:
  * ⭐⭐⭐ **Mastery**: 10/10 perfect score
  * ⭐⭐ **Proficient**: 7–9/10 solid grasp
  * ⭐ **Developing**: 3–6/10 revision recommended

### 🖥️ Authentic CBT Exam Simulation
* **Real Examination Countdown Timer** with automated section alerts.
* **5-Color Status Matrix** Question Palette:
  * ⚪ *Unvisited* | 🔴 *Not Answered* | 🟢 *Answered* | 🟣 *Marked for Review* | 🟣🟢 *Answered & Marked for Review*
* Realistic marking scheme: **+2.0** for correct answers, **-0.5** negative marking penalty.
* Built-in **virtual calculator** and **in-browser scratchpad** for numerical sections.

### 📊 In-Depth Performance Analytics
* **Subject & Topic Diagnosis**: Automatically categorizes strengths and targeted weaknesses.
* **Accuracy vs. Speed Metrics**: Identify guessing habits and time sinks.
* **Full Test Review History**: Detailed explanations and step-by-step solutions for every question.

### 📑 Productivity & UX
* **Revision Vault**: One-click bookmarking to curate personal high-difficulty question lists.
* **Modern Interface**: Fluid transitions, accessible typography, and seamless Light/Dark mode toggling.
